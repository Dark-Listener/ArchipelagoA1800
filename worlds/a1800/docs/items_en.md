## Naming

Item names may be prefixed with shorthands corresponding to the regions in which they're available. If they're available in all regions, there will be no prefix. For example: `OW: Sawmill`, `OW|NW: Rails`

Prefixes:
* `OW:` Old World
* `NW:` New World
* `AR:` The Arctic
* `EN:` Enbesa

## Types of Items

Currently, there are 3 types of items in Anno 1800: Buildings, Expeditions and Recipes.

### Buildings
Buildings are the most common. When received, they can be built (assuming the required resources and workforce are available).

Monument buildings are separated into stages. For example the World's Fair needs 5 separate items to be fully built. The following monuments and stages are currently available:
* World's Fair:
  * World's Fair: Foundations
  * World's Fair: Superstructure
  * World's Fair: Glazing
  * World's Fair: Infrastructure
* Arctic Airship Hangar (only with `The Passage` DLC):
  * Arctic Airship Hangar: Foundations
  * Arctic Airship Hangar: Structure
  * Arctic Airship Hangar: Roof
* Research Institute (only with `Land of Lions` DLC):
  * Research Institute: Foundations
  * Research Institute: Superstructure
* The Iron Tower (only with `Tourist Season` DLC):
  * The Iron Tower: Foundations
  * The Iron Tower: Superstructure
* Skyline Tower (only with `The High Life` DLC):
  * Skyline Tower: Foundations
  * Skyline Tower: Superstructure
  * Skyline Tower: Glazing

### Expeditions
Expeditions are announced by the captain and available on the left side of the screen. These are the normal
expeditions used to unlock the other sessions.

The following expeditions are currently available:
* Expedition: New World
* Expedition: Cape Trelawney (only with `Sunken Treasures` DLC)
* Expedition: The Arctic (only with `The Passage` DLC)
* Expedition: Enbesa (only with `Land of Lions` DLC)

### Recipes
Recipes looks similar to buildings, but correspond to a basic, blank building. Some are always given combined with the blank buildings, others are not. They can be built if the blank variant is also available.

The following combined recipe buildings are available:
* Orchard (only with `Tourist Season` or `The High Life` DLCs)
* Chemical Plant (only with `Tourist Season` or `The High Life` DLCs)
* Assembly Line (only with `The High Life` DLC)
* Artisan's Workshop (only with `The High Life` DLC)
* Hacienda Farm (only with `Seeds of Change` DLC)
* Hacienda Brewery (only with `Seeds of Change` DLC)
* Supply Factory (only with `Empire of the Skies` DLC)

## Starting Items

The player always starts with some of the same items as in vanilla. These are:
* OW: Small Trading Post
* OW: Small Warehouse
* OW: Dirt Road
* OW: Lumberjack's Hut
* OW: Sawmill
* OW: Marketplace
* OW: Farmer Residence
* NW: Small Trading Post
* AR: Small Trading Post (only with `The Passage` DLC)
* AR: Small Sky Trading Post (only with `The Passage` DLC)
* EN: Small Trading Post (only with `Land of Lions` DLC)

## Early Items

A few items are forced to appear early and always in Anno, so the player doesn't get stuck in the early game. These are:
* OW: Fishery
* OW: Sheep Farm
* OW: Framework Knitters
* OW: Fire Station
* OW: Worker Residence
